# iSekur plugins

The plugin catalog of [iSekur](https://github.com/gcanosa/iSekur), the terminal IRC client with a
built-in webchat. Nothing here is installed by default: in iSekur, `/plugin` opens the plugin
window (or the puzzle button in the webchat), and `/plugin install name` fetches one from here,
checks its sha256 against `index.json`, and turns it on.

| Plugin | Version | API | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **autoaway** | 1.4 | 1 | Away when you're idle or nothing is attached, back when you return; on or off per network, away and back announced if you like. |
| **awaylog** | 1.4 | 1 | Your highlights and private messages while you were away or detached, summed up in each network's console when you come back, with a pointer in the tab you're on. |
| **lolcat** | 1.0 | 8 | The messages in the channels and queries where you turn it on, in color on your screens only: nothing is sent in color and the logs keep the plain text. `pattern`: `rainbow`, `theme` (your theme's nick colors), `pastel`, `fire`, `ice`, `forest`, `neon`, `sunset` or `random`; `lines`: everyone's, only yours or only the others'; a color per letter or per word (`by`), `spread` letters per step, and with `shift` the colors flow down the lines like lolcat. On a light theme the colors are darkened to read. `/lolcat` shows every pattern. |
| **nickcolors** | 1.0 | 13 | Nicks in the colors the chat gives them: the nicks said inside a message (a nick of the channel in someone's text gets its color, not inside a link, a `#name` or a text with colors of its own, nor a short one: `min`) and the user list, each nick in its chat color with the rank letter keeping its own. `where`: `both`, `chat` or `userlist`. On your screens only: nothing is sent in color and the logs keep the plain text. You are the theme's own color for you, one none of the others has (iSekur's `/set own_nick_color`), so you can pick yourself out of a crowd. |
| **keepnick** | 1.0 | 4 | Takes your nick back once it's free: when you see its holder leave, and by asking the server every 30 seconds (`every`). A `/nick` you type yourself stops it on that network until you reconnect. |
| **pasteguard** | 1.0 | 4 | A paste of 3 lines or more (`lines`) into a channel or a query goes to a pastebin (paste.rs by default, `service`) and only its link is said. If the upload fails nothing is sent: `/pasteguard send` or `/pasteguard retry`. |
| **autosort** | 1.1 | 6 | Keeps the tabs grouped by network, each network's status tab first, then its channels and queries by name. A tab you `/move` (or drag) stays where you put it and the others sort around it, until iSekur restarts. |
| **tts** | 1.2 | 7 | Reads messages aloud: with this machine's speech engine (`say` on macOS; `spd-say`, `espeak-ng`, `flite` or `festival` on Linux and BSD), in the webchat's browser voice, or both (`where`). `language` (everywhere, or per network or channel) picks the language. Only the networks and channels you enable. `/tts engines` shows which engines this system has; `/tts test` says a line. |
| **screensaver** | 1.2 | 5 | After `idle` minutes (10) without a key, click or mouse move, the terminal and the webchat show digital rain, floating particles or a quiet sky of drifting stars (`saver`: `matrix`, `particles`, `stars` or `random`) until you touch something. With `lock` on, waking asks for the lock password (the one `/lock` sets). Locking iSekur (Alt+S, `/lock`) starts one too, behind the password box (`on_lock` off: it doesn't). Never while nobody is at a terminal or the webchat. `/screensaver [name]` starts one now. |
| **pmguard** | 2.0 | 10 | Private messages from people you haven't let through are held, not opened: you're told once and they get a notice (the `reply` setting, empty for none). `/pmguard allow nick`, or **Allow PMs** in the nicklist's right click, opens the query with what they said; **Remove from PM list** (`/pmguard deny nick`) holds them again. People you `/msg` yourself, services and notices go through; `channels on` lets in people who share a channel with you. Who's let through is the `allowed` setting, per network, so `/plugin`'s window shows and edits it in the terminal and the webchat. |
| **preview** | 1.4 | 2 | Under links people post, in the webchat: a card with the page's title, description and picture, the picture itself for image links, and a player for audio and video links (mp3, ogg, m4a, mp4, webm...). In the terminal, the title and description as text. |
| **push** | 1.0 | 2 | Your highlights and private messages pushed to your phone, tablet or browser while you're away or nothing is attached: ntfy (no account: the ntfy app or ntfy.sh in a browser, `/plugin set push topic <name>`) or a Telegram bot. `/push test` checks it. |
| **proxy** | 1.0 | 3 | Connects the networks you pick through a SOCKS4/SOCKS5 or HTTP proxy, or an SSH jump host (`ssh -W`); the others stay direct. |
| **remind** | 1.0 | 1 | `/remind 10m check the build`, `/remind 1h30m ...`, `/remind 18:30 call home`: shown in that tab and the one you're on; saved, so one due while iSekur was off shows when it starts. `/remind` lists them, `/remind del 2`. |
| **seen** | 1.3 | 1 | `/seen nick`: when and where someone was last seen talking, joining, leaving, quitting or renaming; answers `!seen` where you turn it on. |
| **radio** | 1.1 | 17 | `/radio` opens a radio with four tabs: Recommended (over 100 music stations, YouTube's 24/7 channels (Claude FM, Lofi Girl, State Azure's State FM, Pink Floyd style, jazz, classical, synthwave, 80s) and plain internet radio (SomaFM, Radio Paradise, FIP, Radio Swiss, artist radios...), kept free of dead streams), Browse and Search (about 50,000 stations from the community directory [radio-browser.info](https://www.radio-browser.info): by country, genre, language, top voted and trending; search by name and narrow it by country, genre, language and quality; `Add` keeps one in Mine, and a station that doesn't play says why and the next one in the list is tried) and Mine (your own: kept ones, `/radio add`). Audio only: the webchat plays it in its page, on a phone too, even with the screen locked; the terminal plays it in the background with mpv, ffplay or vlc. `/radio play [station]` (your ★ favorite, else a random one), `stop`, `mute`, `vol [0-150|+n|-n]` (or the slider), `random`, `fav`, `list [text]`, `find <words> [country:AR tag:jazz lang:spanish min:128]`, `top`, `save` (what plays, into Mine), `add <address> [name]` for your own. A cassette's reels turn while it plays and the status bar says `R: ON`. The station list is fetched from [radio/stations.tsv](radio/stations.tsv) every day, and a daily check takes dead streams out of it ([radio/README.md](radio/README.md)). Needs ffmpeg (and yt-dlp for YouTube) on the machine iSekur runs on; `output` picks the webchat, this machine's speakers or both, `source` direct lets the webchat's device connect to plain stations itself, `directory` (`off`: no Browse and Search) names the directory server: its searches and the stations you play are known to it. |
| **zen** | 1.0 | 11 | `/zen` hides the tabs, the nicklist, the topic and the status bar and leaves only the chat, in a column in the middle (`width`), the last `lines` (5) bright and the ones before them dimmed, so you keep the thread without the noise. It draws in `mono` (or `mono-light`, or `keep` your theme); nothing is saved, so `/zen` again, Esc on an empty line, or disabling the plugin brings back your theme and layout. `where`: `both`, `terminal` or `web`; joins and parts hidden unless `events` is on. |
| **eventshidemgr** | 1.2 | 14 | Runs of joins, parts, quits, nick changes and, when you switch them on, hostname changes, mode changes, kicks and topic changes fold into one row, `› 3 joined, 2 left, 1 mode set` in the terminal and `⇄ 3 joined, 2 left, 1 mode set ▸` in the webchat. A click opens the run and folds it again, and so does leaving the tab. You pick the kinds, one setting each in the plugin's page: `joins`, `leaves`, `nicks` (on), `hosts`, `modes`, `kicks`, `topics` (off); a kind that doesn't fold stays a plain line and ends the run, and hostname changes only show as lines where `hosts` is on. Your own events and every message end a run; a lone event stays a normal line. `names` lists who (`3 joined (a, b, +1)`; the webchat's tooltip always does). `/quiet` hides joins, parts, quits, nick and host changes and mode changes. In a `/quiet` tab nothing shows, as always, unless `quiet` is `fold`: then the summary rows stay there. On your screens only: the logs keep every line. |
| **twilight** | 1.1 | 15 | Warms the colors after dark, like f.lux or Night Shift, in the terminal and the webchat and whatever the theme. Night is sunset to sunrise where *you* are (`location`: a city, or `latitude,longitude`; iSekur on a server in another country still follows your sun and your clock) or the hours you pick (`mode`, `start`, `end`). `day` and `night` temperatures in kelvin, extra `dim`, a `ramp` of minutes around sunset and sunrise, and a `fade` (60 s) for switching it on or off, a manual change or the end of a pause, and `media` (on) to tint the webchat's pictures and videos too (the preview plugin's): **the screen never jumps**, so disabling it at night glides back to bright too. `/twilight` opens a window with a sun and a moon, a **Preview** button (the same sun, moon and buttons head its page in `/plugin`, Configure, so you can try a setting as you change it) (`/twilight preview [seconds]` sweeps day to night and back) and `Off 1h`; `/twilight 2700` a manual temperature, `darkroom` red only, `off [minutes]`, `auto`, `location <city>`. The location goes to open-meteo.com (a city's coordinates, then the sun's times); nothing else leaves this machine. |
| **dropzone** | 1.3 | 16 | Give iSekur a file and it goes to a file host, and a box asks what to do with the link: send it to this tab, copy it only, or send it to another channel. Drag a file onto the webchat (before you let go the page shows what will happen: a frame around the page that says "Drop to upload"), paste a screenshot into it, tap the paperclip on a phone, drop a file on the terminal (it types the path, and a line that is only a file's path goes to the plugin) or `/upload file` (`/upload` alone opens the browser's file picker). `service`: the hosts to try, in order, the next one when a host says no (file hosts often refuse a server's address, and the file leaves from the machine iSekur runs on): `litterbox uguu` by default; a host that says no to your machine is left out for an hour (the box says so), so a file isn't sent twice, and the messages say how long a link lasts. `litterbox` (no account; the link goes away after `expire`: 1h, 12h, 24h or 72h; files up to 1 GB), `uguu` (no account; links last 3 hours; up to 128 MB), `catbox` (kept; 200 MB), `imgur` (images and video; your own Client-ID in `imgur_id`) or the `https://` address of your own that takes a form POST and answers with the link (`field`). `action`: `ask` (the box), `send` (the link goes to the tab at once) or `copy`, per channel. The link is public, whoever has it can open the file, and the box names the host that gets the file and says that it is a third party (iSekur has nothing to do with it, the plugin only automates the sharing), has a field for an optional message that goes before the link, and a tab with end-to-end encryption says in it that the file will not be. Nothing leaves your machine before you answer. A failed upload is kept for half an hour: `/dropzone retry`; when every host refuses the machine iSekur runs on (a 412, 403 or 429, or no answer) the message says so and points to `service`. |

```
/plugin available
/plugin install seen
/plugin set seen@sekurnet reply on
/plugin update
```

All of these are official: made for iSekur by its author, Gerardo Canosa. Each one's About (in the
plugin windows) says who made a plugin and how to reach them.

Plugins are Lua 5.4, each in its own sandbox: no files, programs or sockets of their own (`isekur.http` fetches web pages for them, never from your LAN).
[WRITING.md](WRITING.md) is the guide for writing one (events, the `isekur` API, settings,
the catalog's rules). New plugins and fixes are welcome as pull requests; `python3 check.py`
must pass.

GPL-3.0-or-later, like iSekur.
